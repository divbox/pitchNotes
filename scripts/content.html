Arsenal went to the Amex chasing an eighth straight win to open the season, something the club hadn't managed in more than a century, and came home with their first defeat instead. Brighton were 3-0 up and cruising: Pascal Groß curled in the opener just past the half-hour, Charalampos Kostoulas made it two on the stroke of half-time, and Chema Andrés headed a third from a corner before the hour. The champions barely laid a glove on them.

The result knocks Arsenal off top spot, still level with Manchester City on points but now behind on goal difference (more on that in Divbox 101). And spare a thought for Manchester United: 13th, one win in four league games, and a manager already under fire after surrendering a two-goal lead at home to Brighton in the League Cup in midweek. Michael Carrick takes his side to Fulham on Sunday needing something, anything, to quiet the noise.

This weekend keeps the normal rhythm, Arsenal on Saturday and Manchester United at Fulham on Sunday, but it's the last league football for three weeks. The September and October international breaks have been merged into one long pause this season, so once Matchweek 5 finishes on the 20th, the Premier League doesn't return until Matchweek 6 on 10 October. Arsenal and Manchester City are both sitting on 12 points. City are first, Arsenal second. The thing splitting them is goal difference: goals scored minus goals conceded across the whole season so far. City are on +6, Arsenal on +4, so City take the higher spot despite the identical points.

It's why today's 3-0 stung twice. Arsenal dropped three points they fancied taking, and their goal difference slid by three at the same time, enough to flip them from first to second in an afternoon. Two clubs can pile up the same points all season, but the one that wins big and loses small edges ahead on goal difference, and come May that quietly kept column is what separates teams level on points, at the top of the table and the bottom of it. Something to remember the next time a side keeps attacking at 4-0 up. Those extra goals aren't showing off, they're insurance.
