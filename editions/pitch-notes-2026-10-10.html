Back from the break, back from behind

Three weeks without league football, and Arsenal spent the first hour of the restart looking like a side still on holiday. It was goalless at half-time, then Leeds scored about ten minutes into the second half and the Emirates had the Brighton defeat on its mind again. The reply came quickly. Riccardo Calafiori equalised at the back post on 62 minutes, Mikel Arteta sent on three substitutes, and one of them, Bruno Guimarães, drilled a low shot in from outside the box on 70. (More on the substitutes in Divbox 101.)

A warning on timing. This edition went out with the game in its closing minutes and Arsenal 2-1 up, so that score is not a final one. If it holds, Arsenal draw level with Manchester City on 15 points having played a game more, and City go to Liverpool on Sunday. Manchester United play later today at home to Tottenham, 12th against 20th. United have one win in five league games. Spurs have none, and two goals all season.

Arsenal & Man United

Club News

ARS Arsenal

Arsenal went into the break on the back of the 3-0 at Brighton, and for an hour against Leeds it looked like the hangover had lasted all three weeks. Calafiori and Guimarães turned it round after the hour, and Arsenal were 2-1 ahead as this edition went out. William Saliba is out until December with a back problem, according to Sports Illustrated, so Ezri Konsa started next to Gabriel in the middle of the defence. Lille visit in the Champions League on Tuesday.

MUN Man United

United come back from the break 12th with five points, and the treatment room is full. Michael Carrick has ruled out Patrick Dorgu, who has a hamstring injury and will miss several weeks, and Benjamin Sesko, who has a shin problem. Manuel Ugarte is still recovering from knee surgery and Matthijs de Ligt has a back injury. Marcus Rashford, Kobbie Mainoo and Amad Diallo have done only light training. The good news is Carlos Baleba, who is fit enough for a possible debut against Tottenham. Away from the pitch, Antonio Conte told The Athletic he wants a club with a big history that is going through a hard time. Fabrizio Romano read that as a message to United among others, though nobody has reported an approach.

Substitutions, and how a bench changes a game

Divbox 101

Arsenal trailed Leeds, equalised, and then Arteta sent on three substitutes. One of them, Bruno Guimarães, put Arsenal ahead a few minutes after coming on.

Each Premier League team names nine substitutes and can use five of them. The catch is that a manager can only stop the game three times to make changes, so swapping two or three players in one go saves stoppages for later. Changes at half-time don't count against the three. A player who comes off can't go back on. There are also extra substitutions for suspected concussions, and those sit outside the five.

Five substitutes suits clubs with deep squads. Arsenal could bring on Guimarães, Jurriën Timber and Viktor Gyökeres against Leeds, and few teams in the league have a bench like that. Smaller clubs argued against the rule for that reason before it came in for good in 2022.
