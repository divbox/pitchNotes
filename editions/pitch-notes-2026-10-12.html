Ahead against ten men, and still no win

Manchester United had Tottenham beaten on Saturday and let them go. Bryan Mbeumo scored on 73 minutes from a Matheus Cunha pass. Five minutes later Rodrigo Bentancur head-butted Luke Shaw and the referee sent him off. United had a goal and an extra man for the last 12 minutes plus stoppage time, against a side that had scored twice all season. In the second minute of stoppage time Dominic Solanke headed in a Sandro Tonali cross and it finished 1-1. ESPN reported boos at half-time and again at the end. United are 13th with six points and one win from six league games.

Arsenal's 2-1 over Leeds held, which the last edition couldn't promise because it went out before the final whistle. It didn't bring them level with Manchester City for long. City won 3-0 at Liverpool on Sunday, have six wins from six and lead on 18 points. Arsenal are second on 15, two ahead of Brighton. One game of matchweek 6 is left, Coventry against Newcastle tonight. Coventry led 1-0 in the second half as this went out, so that score isn't final.

Arsenal & Man United

Club News

ARS Arsenal

The Leeds win cost Arsenal two players after the final whistle. Piero Hincapié hurt an ankle and Martin Zubimendi took a knock in the warm-down on the pitch, according to Sports Mole. Both had been on the bench, both missed Monday's training, and neither is expected to face Lille. Christos Tzolis is out for a few weeks with a hamstring injury from the international break. William Saliba is training on his own as he recovers from his back problem, and his return is still weeks away. With Hincapié out, Sports Mole expects Riccardo Calafiori to start at left-back again three days after scoring against Leeds, unless Arteta moves Myles Lewis-Skelly there from midfield.

MUN Man United

Michael Carrick's summary after Tottenham was that United are making it difficult for themselves, and that a team in front has to see the game out. Sky Sports called it the club's worst league start in 40 years and said his room for error is shrinking. Carlos Baleba was the bright spot. He came on for Youri Tielemans after an hour for his debut and completed 16 of his 18 passes. Now comes Atlético away, and Atlético have everyone fit. United don't. ESPN lists Benjamin Sesko, Patrick Dorgu, Noussair Mazraoui, Manuel Ugarte and Matthijs de Ligt as out. Luke Shaw and Kobbie Mainoo missed Monday's training, and the Manchester Evening News says the club will decide late on whether they travel.

Red cards, and what they cost afterwards

Divbox 101

Rodrigo Bentancur was sent off on 78 minutes at Old Trafford for head-butting Luke Shaw. Tottenham played the rest of the game with ten men, because a team can't replace a player who has been sent off. They equalised anyway.

The card costs more than the minutes that are left. A second yellow card means missing the next game. A straight red brings a ban that depends on the offence. Denying a clear goalscoring chance is one game. Violent conduct, which covers a head-butt, is normally three, and so is a tackle dangerous enough to count as serious foul play. A player sent off for a second time in the same season gets an extra game on top.

The ban applies to domestic games, so a red card in the league doesn't keep a player out of the Champions League. A club can appeal a straight red if it thinks the referee got it wrong, but it can't appeal a second yellow. If the standard ban applies, Bentancur's first missed game is Coventry at home on Monday 19 October.
