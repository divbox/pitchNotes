Arsenal win ugly at Forest, United get a point and a little breathing room

Arsenal had most of the ball at the City Ground and did very little with it. Neither side had a shot on target in the first half. Arteta took Eberechi Eze off at half-time and sent on Noni Madueke. He said he wanted "a different sort of profile" on the left. With about ten minutes left, Declan Rice found Madueke, whose shot hit Forest's Jair Cunha and went past Matz Sels. Forest had three good chances late on and missed them all. It finished 1-0. Arsenal stay second on 18 points, three behind Manchester City, who beat Ipswich 3-2 on Saturday. Brighton beat Crystal Palace 5-0 and are third on 16.

United went to Leeds after a week of headlines about Lisandro Martinez and the board, and they played better. Bryan Mbeumo scored from a Youri Tielemans cross after 35 minutes. Dominic Calvert-Lewin equalised six minutes into the second half after a corner was headed down to him, and Leeds were the better side from then on. It ended 1-1. Carrick called it "a bit of a fresh start", but he added that nobody was getting carried away, because United still hadn't won. They have one league win from seven games and sit 13th on 7 points. According to Sky Sports, that's United's fewest wins at this stage of a top-flight season since 1986-87.

Arsenal & Man United

Club News

ARS Arsenal

There's no official injury update since Forest. Arteta's pre-Bayern press conference should bring one. Martin Zubimendi is reported to have got over his ankle problem. William Saliba's back and Piero Hincapié's ankle are still keeping them out, and reports expect both back some time in November. The bigger talking point is Eze. He came off at half-time at Forest, and Madueke went on and scored the winner. Arteta said Eze "had some really good moments" but the game needed something different. Jurriën Timber came off the bench for his 100th Arsenal appearance. After the game Arteta said winning away at Villa, Sunderland and Forest, which Arsenal didn't manage last season, "shows how difficult becoming a champion is".

MUN Man United

Martinez started at Leeds, days after his comments about the club, and went off at half-time with a thigh problem. Noussair Mazraoui replaced him. LiveScore reports that Carrick said Marcus Rashford has been managing a hip problem, which is why he started on the bench. Carlos Baleba has started the last two games after his ankle injury, and Gary Neville said on Sky that his fitness is "absolutely critical". Neville also said the Leeds draw could be a stepping stone. He added that if United are still 13th in January, Carrick has a problem. The Conte talk hasn't gone away. Some reports claimed he'd already agreed to take over. Fabrizio Romano said Conte "has no agreement with any club", but also said Conte would now be willing to take a job in mid-season.

Why fifth might be enough, and might not

Divbox 101

When Gary Neville judged Carrick this week, he didn't say "top four". He said anything below fifth would be a disappointment, because missing the top five means missing Champions League money. That's because of a rule that's only been around for a couple of seasons.

The Premier League's top four always qualify for the Champions League. Since the competition expanded in 2024-25, UEFA has given one extra place each to the two countries whose clubs did best in Europe that season. The extra place goes to the next team in the league table. England earned it in 2024-25, so fifth place got in.

That place isn't guaranteed. It depends on how all the English clubs do in Europe this season, so United's results in Como and against Roma help decide whether fifth is enough in May. Right now United are 13th, five points behind Liverpool in fifth. That gap matters more than the rule does.
